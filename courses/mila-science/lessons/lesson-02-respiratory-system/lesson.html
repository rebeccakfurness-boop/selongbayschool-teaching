Breathing IN vs. Breathing OUT

Your ribs and diaphragm move differently depending on whether you're breathing in or out. Compare the two.

Breathing IN — ribs move out, diaphragm flattens and pulls down, lungs get bigger.

Breathing OUT — ribs move in, diaphragm relaxes and domes up, lungs get smaller.

Put your hands on your ribs right now and take a big breath in — can you feel them move?

Where Lesson 1 meets Lesson 2

Handing It Over to the Blood

Remember blood, from last lesson? This is where it picks up its cargo. Inside your lungs, oxygen from the air moves into your blood. At the very same moment, carbon dioxide — a waste gas your body doesn't need — moves out of your blood and into your lungs, ready to be breathed out.

Then your heart (Lesson 1!) pumps that oxygen-rich blood all the way round your body, to every single cell.

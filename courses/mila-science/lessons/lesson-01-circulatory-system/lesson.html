New idea

Your Heart Has Two Sides

Your heart isn't just one pump, it's really two pumps working together, side by side.

⬅️ Left side
Pumps blood WITH oxygen all around your whole body.
➡️ Right side
Pumps blood WITHOUT oxygen to your lungs, to pick more up.

Keep that in mind: it'll matter again when we look at the loop later!

Why it matters

Why Does It Never Stop?

💪 Your heart is a muscle
🔁 Its one job: pump blood, all day, all night
⏱️ It never stops, not even while you sleep
❤️ Every squeeze = one heartbeat

What do you think would happen to the rest of your body if it ever did stop, even for a few seconds?

Feel Your Own Pulse

Every heartbeat pushes blood through your body with a little pulse of pressure, you can actually feel it!

Two fingers, gently, on your wrist or the side of your neck. Stay still. Feel that little beat? That's your pulse.

Count the beats for 15 seconds, then ×4: that's roughly your heart rate for one minute. Faster or slower than you guessed?

New idea

Meet the Blood Vessels

Blood travels through tubes called blood vessels, a bit like water through pipes.

➡️ Arteries: carry blood away from the heart
⬅️ Veins: carry blood back to the heart
🔗 Capillaries: tiny tubes connecting the two, where delivery actually happens

Quick guess: right after a heartbeat, blood gets pushed out hard. Which tube needs thicker, stronger walls: the one right next to that push, or the one on the calm journey back?

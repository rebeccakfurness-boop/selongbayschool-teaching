Factors of Production
How does Tom's Print Co. actually make stuff?
🖨️ 🧩 🎾
Every business needs 4 ingredients
Tom's Print Co. 3D-prints custom padel grips and Lego-compatible parts. Before we name the ingredients — what do you think you'd actually need to start it this weekend?
Factor 1
🌍
Land
Natural resources used in production — not just soil. The filament spool is made from petroleum. That's land.

Reward: Rent
Factor 2
🧑‍🔧
Labour
Human physical or mental effort. Tom designing CAD files, running the printer, packaging orders.

Reward: Wages
Factor 3
🖨️
Capital
Manufactured goods used to produce other goods. The printer, the laptop, the tools.

Not money! Cash isn't capital in economics.

Reward: Interest
Factor 4
🎲
Enterprise
Risk-taking + organising the other three factors. Tom's decision to start the business at all.

Reward: Profit — the only one that isn't guaranteed.
Quantity vs Quality

📈 Quantity

Tom buys a second 3D printer.
More units of the same capital.

⭐ Quality

Tom upgrades to a faster, more precise printer.
Same number, better ones.

Quick check
🎾 The padel club buys 3 more courts. Quantity or quality of capital?

🎾 The head coach completes an advanced coaching qualification. Quantity or quality of labour?
Recap
🌍 Land → Rent
🧑‍🔧 Labour → Wages
🖨️ Capital → Interest (not money!)
🎲 Enterprise → Profit (not guaranteed)

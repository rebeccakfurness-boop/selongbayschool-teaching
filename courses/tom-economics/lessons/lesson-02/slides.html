Disequilibrium & Price Changes
Why can't you ever get a padel court at 6pm?
Quick recap
At equilibrium, quantity demanded = quantity supplied. You already know this cold — today's about what happens when it isn't equal.
🎾 6–8pm slot: SHORTAGE
Club's normal price (P1) is below the true equilibrium for this slot. Quantity demanded (Qd) > quantity supplied (Qs, fixed courts). That gap is the shortage.
🎾 9am slot: SURPLUS
Same price (P1) is now above the true equilibrium for this quiet slot. Quantity supplied (Qs) > quantity demanded (Qd). Empty courts = surplus.
Clearing it: pricing
Raise the evening price → quantity demanded falls back (movement along D) until it matches supply.

Cut the morning price → quantity demanded rises until it matches supply.

Both cases: price moves toward that slot's own equilibrium.
🧩 📈
Same idea: retired Lego sets
Set is discontinued → supply fixed/shrinking, demand persists → shortage at old retail price → resale price rises until quantity demanded falls to match the fixed supply.
Causes vs consequences
Cause of a price change → a shift in demand and/or supply.

Consequence of a price change → quantity bought & sold changes.

Watch out: a price change itself only moves you along a curve — it doesn't shift the curve.

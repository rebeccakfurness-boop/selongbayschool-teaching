New idea

Introducing PES

Price elasticity of supply (PES) measures how responsive quantity supplied is to a change in price — exactly the question we just asked about courts vs. grips.

PES = %ΔQs ÷ %ΔP
Same shape as the PED formula, but for sellers, not buyers. One key difference: PES is (almost) always positive — the law of supply says price and quantity supplied move in the same direction, so there's no minus sign to ignore this time.
